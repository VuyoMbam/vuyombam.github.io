Areas of Expertise

Techno-economic Analysis

Skilled in modelling energy yield performance of multiple energy technologies and discounted cashflow analysis (DCF) to investigate financial metrics such as the NPV, IRR, and payback period to inform investment decisions and policy recommendations.

Energy System Modelling

Long-term energy system planning using capacity mix projection models, including OSeMOSYS and bespoke tools, and power system dispatch modelling, with analysis of the resulting dispatch and system marginal prices.

Macroeconomic Impact Modelling

Development of input-output models (multiplier-based) to model macroeconomic impacts (jobs, GDP, etc.) of in energy projects throughout their operational life.

More about Me

I am an energy sector analyst and advisor at Kuungana Advisory Limited, where I lead workstreams on energy system modelling for South Africa and other countries in the Southern African Power Pool (SAPP). I combine technical and financial modelling to turn complex analysis into clear insights that inform transactions, route-to-market strategies and investment decisions for project developers, power traders, utilities and financial institutions. I also contribute to energy policy and regulation projects in SAPP member countries and beyond, mostly in low- and middle-income countries.

Previously, I was an energy economist at the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), where my projects included:

  • a Clean Cooking Roadmap for Mpumalanga province
  • an Offshore Wind Roadmap for South Africa
  • an analysis of localisation opportunities in the solar PV and battery energy storage value chains
  • an assessment of how ready South Africa’s education and training ecosystem is for a green hydrogen economy
